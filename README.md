# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis | Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive sales analysis dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze coffee shop transactions, identify sales trends, and generate actionable business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and Power Pivot
- DAX Measures
- GETPIVOTDATA
- Interactive Slicers and Charts

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Sales Measures:** Total Sales, Total Footfall, Average Bill per Person, and Average Order per Person.
- **Hourly Analysis:** Analyze order quantities across different hours.
- **Category Analysis:** Compare revenue across product categories.
- **Order Size Distribution:** Analyze orders by size.
- **Store Comparison:** Compare sales revenue and transaction counts across locations.
- **Top 5 Products:** Identify products generating the highest revenue.
- **Weekday Analysis:** Compare order quantities across different days.
- **Interactive Filters:** Filter data by month and day of the week.

## 🔍 Key Analysis Questions & Findings

**1. How do sales vary by day and hour?**

Order activity peaks around 10 AM, with strong activity between 8 AM and 9 AM.

**2. What are the peak sales hours?**

The highest hourly order quantity occurs at 10 AM, suggesting a need for effective staffing and inventory planning during morning hours.

**3. What is the monthly sales revenue?**

June records the highest revenue at $166,485.88, while February records the lowest at $76,145.19.

**4. How do stores perform?**

Hell's Kitchen leads with $236,511.17 in sales, followed by Astoria ($232,243.91) and Lower Manhattan ($230,057.25).

**5. What is the average bill and order quantity?**

- Average Bill per Person: $4.69
- Average Order per Person: 1.44

**6. Which products generate the highest revenue?**

- Barista Espresso — $91,406.20
- Brewed Chai Tea — $77,081.95
- Hot Chocolate — $72,416.00
- Gourmet Brewed Coffee — $70,034.60
- Brewed Black Tea — $47,932.00

**7. Which categories perform best?**

Coffee generates the highest category revenue ($269,952.45), followed by Tea ($196,405.95) and Bakery ($82,315.64).

Order-size analysis shows Regular at 31%, Large at 30%, Standard at 30%, and Small at 9%.

## 💡 Key Insights

- Morning hours show the highest order activity.
- June is the strongest month by revenue.
- Hell's Kitchen is the highest-performing store by sales.
- Coffee is the leading revenue-generating category.
- Barista Espresso is the top product by revenue.
